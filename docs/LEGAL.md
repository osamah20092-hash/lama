# Legal & originality notes

This project is **original work**. To keep it that way:

- **Do not** copy code, decompiled output, audio, images, fonts, animations, character
  designs, logos, or room/UI layouts from any existing commercial app.
- The games included (e.g. **Ludo / Jackaroo**) are traditional, public-domain board games.
  Implement the rules yourself; do not import another app's assets or art for them.
- Create your own name, logo, color palette, sounds, and avatars — or use assets you have
  a license to (Unity Asset Store, CC0 libraries, commissioned art).
- Third-party SDKs (Firebase, Agora) are used under their own official licenses and terms.
  Register your own accounts and use your own keys.
- Pick an open-source license for your own code (MIT is included as a suggestion).

Functional concepts — "voice rooms", "seats", "gifts", "in-room games" — are common to the
whole genre and are not owned by any one app. Your expression (art, code, content) is what
you own and must keep original.
